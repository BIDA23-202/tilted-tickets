About Tilted Tickets

Tilted Tickets is a Botswana-first event ticketing platform for concerts, theatre, campus events, sports and conferences. It solves a measurable local problem: event discovery is scattered across WhatsApp groups and Facebook pages, ticket sales are manual, and screenshot tickets are trivially faked at the gate.

The problem

  • No single trusted platform covers Botswana events across all cities.
  • Organisers chase payments via WhatsApp and manage lists in Excel.
  • Screenshot tickets cannot be validated reliably at the gate.
  • No local-payment-native checkout — most platforms default to foreign cards.

Our solution

  • One platform to discover and book events across Gaborone, Francistown, Maun and beyond.
  • Orange Money and MyZaka at checkout — no foreign card required.
  • Unique QR codes per attendee, validated once and marked used on scan.
  • Organiser real-time dashboard with analytics, CSV export and gate check-in.

Revenue model

7%Service fee per ticket
3%Transaction fee per ticket
90%Paid out to organiser

At 500 tickets sold at BWP 250 average, Tilted Tickets earns BWP 12,500. Scale to 10,000 tickets annually and the model is self-sustaining without grants or external funding.
