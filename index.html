About Tilted Tickets

Tilted Tickets is a Botswana-focused event ticketing platform for concerts, theatre, campus events, sports and conferences. The platform solves a measurable local problem: event discovery is scattered, ticket sales are manual, and gate validation with screenshots is trivially faked.

Problem we are solving

  • No single trusted platform covers Botswana events across cities.
  • Organisers chase payments via WhatsApp and manage guest lists in Excel.
  • Screenshot tickets are impossible to validate reliably at the gate.
  • No local-payment-native checkout — most platforms default to foreign cards.

Our solution

  • One platform for buyers to discover and book events across Gaborone, Francistown, Maun and beyond.
  • Orange Money and MyZaka at checkout — no foreign card required.
  • Unique QR codes per attendee, validated once and invalidated on scan.
  • Organisers get a real-time dashboard, attendee export, and gate check-in tool.

Revenue model

7%Service fee per ticket
3%Transaction fee per ticket
90%Paid to the organiser

At 500 tickets sold at BWP 250 each, Tilted Tickets earns BWP 12,500 in fees. Scale to 10,000 tickets and the model is self-sustaining. Premium organiser tiers and sponsored event placement are additional revenue streams at scale.

Go-to-market plan

  • Phase 1 (now): Prototype and academic validation. Onboard 3–5 Gaborone event organisers as pilot partners.
  • Phase 2 (6 months): Live Orange Money and MyZaka API integration. Launch on GitHub Pages with real payment rails.
  • Phase 3 (12 months): Expand to Francistown and Maun. Add mobile app wrapper. Target 50,000 annual tickets processed.
